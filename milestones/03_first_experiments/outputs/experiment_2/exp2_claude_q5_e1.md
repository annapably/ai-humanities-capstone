**Answer:**

The excerpt records **12 interjections from FPÖ members**: 11 with their wording and 1 without (Petschnig). They come from five named members (Darmann, Kaniak, Wurm, Petschnig, Steiner) and two unnamed calls from the FPÖ benches ("Ruf bei der FPÖ").

**On gender:** the protocol uses the abbreviation "Abg." without stating gender. Gender is only marked where the text itself shows it. Holzegger is female ("Abgeordnete Ines Holzegger", "Frau Kollegin"). Wurm is male ("zum Kollegen Wurm").

**1.** "(Abg. Darmann [FPÖ] – erheitert –: Ja, das ist die übliche Praxis in einem Landgasthaus!)"
- **Caller:** Darmann, gender unclear, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** implicitly the speaker, Holzegger (NEOS); no direct address
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** It comes right after her scenario of a gold buyer sitting in a country inn. The note "erheitert" (amused) and the exaggerated "übliche Praxis" ("usual practice") suggest he is not seriously agreeing. It reads as ironic, mocking how unlikely the scenario sounds.

**2.** "(Abg. Kaniak [FPÖ]: Für Freiheit! Merk dir das, ein Wort reicht: Freiheit! …)"
- **Caller:** Kaniak, gender unclear, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** Holzegger, using the informal "dir" (du-form)
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** A direct answer to her question about what the FPÖ stands for. "Merk dir das" ("remember that") and "ein Wort reicht" ("one word is enough") give it a lecturing tone. Repeating "Freiheit" is emphatic. It reads as a genuine statement of position, delivered pointedly.

**3.** "(… – Ruf bei der FPÖ: Bei uns hat alles Platz! Wir sind Volkspartei!)"
- **Caller:** unclear (unnamed call from the FPÖ benches); gender unclear
- **Addressed:** implicitly Holzegger
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** This picks up her claim of contradictory positions and turns it into a claim of breadth ("there's room for everything with us"). The text doesn't show whether it is meant seriously or humorously.

**4.** "(Ruf bei der FPÖ: Das ist nur eine Verständnisfrage!)"
- **Caller:** unclear; gender unclear
- **Addressed:** unclear, probably Holzegger
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** It follows her remark about "mixed signals" from "Wurm und Kolm". It suggests the contradiction is only a matter of her understanding. Whether "Verständnisfrage" means her comprehension or a mere question of interpretation is unclear from the text.

**5.** "(… – Abg. Kaniak [FPÖ]: Für diese Empfindungsstörungen können wir aber nichts!)"
- **Caller:** Kaniak, gender unclear, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** Holzegger
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** This answers her "vielleicht entscheiden Sie sich da einmal" ("maybe make up your minds"). It calls her perception a "disorder" ("Empfindungsstörungen") and so puts the problem on her side rather than the FPÖ's. The tone is sarcastic and dismissive.

**6.** "(Abg. Wurm [FPÖ]: Das ist relativ unkompliziert!)"
- **Caller:** Wurm, male, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** implicitly Holzegger
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** It comes as she moves to the motion on three decimal places at petrol stations, presumably his own motion, since she criticises "Kollege Wurm". He pre-emptively describes the issue as simple. It reads as genuine.

**7.** "(Zwischenruf des Abg. Petschnig [FPÖ].)"
- **Caller:** Petschnig, gender unclear, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** unclear
- **Type:** interjection, but its wording is not recorded
- **Interpretation:** The text is not sufficient to say anything about the content or tone.

**8.** "(Abg. Wurm [FPÖ]: Genau!)"
- **Caller:** Wurm, male, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** Holzegger
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** It confirms her statement that the motion is based on a 2009 Arbö press release. It reads as genuine agreement with that fact, not with her criticism.

**9.** "(Abg. Wurm [FPÖ]: Das haben Sie nicht genau gelesen, Frau Kollegin!)"
- **Caller:** Wurm, male, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** Holzegger, explicitly as "Frau Kollegin" (a form of address to the speaker)
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** He contradicts her reading that the press release warns of price increases. He uses the formal "Sie" and accuses her of not reading carefully.

**10.** "(Abg. Wurm [FPÖ]: Nein, aber andere Lokalwährungen!)"
- **Caller:** Wurm, male, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** Holzegger
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** He replies to her own sarcastic remark that she didn't know so many European countries had the Schilling. He takes her sarcasm literally and corrects it on substance: other countries had other local currencies.

**11.** "(Abg. Steiner [FPÖ]: Es hat die D-Mark gegeben!)"
- **Caller:** Steiner, gender unclear, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** Holzegger
- **Type:** interjection
- **Interpretation:** This backs up Wurm's previous interjection with a concrete example. It reads as genuine, and it also counters her "vielleicht habe ich mich da einfach geirrt" ("maybe I was simply mistaken"), which was itself ironic.

**12.** "(… – Abg. Wurm [FPÖ]: Frau Kollegin, Sie haben nichts über das Gold gesagt!)"
- **Caller:** Wurm, male, FPÖ
- **Addressed:** Holzegger, explicitly as "Frau Kollegin"
- **Type:** interjection, after the speech has ended
- **Interpretation:** He claims her speech did not deal with the gold topic. Holzegger contradicts him directly as she walks back to her seat: "Natürlich hab ich etwas übers Gold gesagt!" ("Of course I said something about gold!"). That reply is hers (NEOS), not an FPÖ interjection.
