Holzegger (NEOS, female) opens with formal address to the chair and house, "Vielen herzlichen Dank, Herr Präsident! Werte Frau Staatssekretärin! Hohes Haus!", and is addressed by FPÖ members in the same register: Abg. Wurm (FPÖ) calls her "Frau Kollegin, Sie haben nichts über das Gold gesagt!", a polite form with a reproachful, corrective tone. The interjections are mostly ironic or challenging: Abg. Darmann (FPÖ), marked "erheitert", answers her anecdote with "Ja, das ist die übliche Praxis in einem Landgasthaus!", which reads as sarcastic, and Abg. Kaniak (FPÖ) uses the informal imperative "Merk dir das, ein Wort reicht: Freiheit!" and later "Für diese Empfindungsstörungen können wir aber nichts!", both addressed to her and both mocking or assertive. The excerpt contains no call to order, and the content of Matznetter's (SPÖ) and Petschnig's (FPÖ) interjections is unclear because only the fact of the interjection is recorded.
