Rules:

4. If something is unclear (e.g. a missing name or party), write "unclear" and do not guess.
5. If the text is not sufficient to answer the question, say so explicitly.
6. Describe language only. Do not evaluate persons or parties.
7. When needed, also consider whether an interjection / reaction is genuine or implicitly sarcastic.

Yes, the excerpt contains **14 interjections**. All of them are directed at the speaker, **Abgeordnete Ines Holzegger (NEOS, female)**, or at her statements. Gender is only stated in the text for her and, indirectly, for Wurm. For everyone else it is "unclear". I did not use the project files, because the answer rests only on the excerpt.

**Interjections**

1. "(Zwischenruf des Abg. Matznetter [SPÖ].)"
   - Caller: Matznetter, SPÖ, gender unclear.
   - The content is not given in the text.
2. "(Abg. Darmann [FPÖ] – erheitert –: Ja, das ist die übliche Praxis in einem Landgasthaus!)"
   - Caller: Darmann, FPÖ, gender unclear.
   - This reacts to her gasthaus scenario. The stage direction "erheitert" and the phrasing suggest an ironic remark rather than a literal agreement.
3. "(Ruf bei der SPÖ: Was?)"
   - Caller: unnamed, SPÖ, gender unclear.
   - This is a reaction to her remark about a system "wo der Staat maximal eingreift". It reads as surprise or objection.
4. "(Abg. Kaniak [FPÖ]: Für Freiheit! Merk dir das, ein Wort reicht: Freiheit! …)"
   - Caller: Kaniak, FPÖ, gender unclear.
   - This answers her question about what the FPÖ stands for, and he uses the informal "du" with her.
5. "(… – Ruf bei der FPÖ: Bei uns hat alles Platz! Wir sind Volkspartei!)"
   - Caller: unnamed, FPÖ, gender unclear.
   - This is a second call-out in the same bracket, in response to the same question.
6. "(Ruf bei der FPÖ: Das ist nur eine Verständnisfrage!)"
   - Caller: unnamed, FPÖ, gender unclear.
   - This answers her remark about "gemischte Signale". It is a defensive or deflecting reply.
7. "(Abg. Kaniak [FPÖ]: Für diese Empfindungsstörungen können wir aber nichts!)"
   - Caller: Kaniak, FPÖ, gender unclear.
   - It follows her "entscheiden Sie sich da einmal". It is likely sarcastic, since "Empfindungsstörungen" is ironic wording.
8. "(Abg. Wurm [FPÖ]: Das ist relativ unkompliziert!)"
   - Caller: Wurm, FPÖ, male. The text says "Kollegen Wurm" and I take that to be the same person.

9. "(Zwischenruf des Abg. Petschnig [FPÖ].)"
